Why a standard, not a bigger spreadsheet

Every lender's loan management system exports data in its own shape. Every destination — CBN's CRMS, a credit bureau, an IFRS 9 model, an investor's diligence team — wants a different one. Without a canonical middle layer, every new integration is a bespoke mapping project, done from scratch, with no shared audit trail. WCDS is that middle layer.

One schema, not a schema per loan type

A deliberate design choice: WCDS doesn't define a separate table for consumer loans, SME loans, and MFB loans. It's one Facility entity — loan-type differences show up in a handful of fields (facility_type, product_code, purpose_code, sector_code), not a different shape. That's what makes cross-lender, cross-product comparison possible in the first place. Below: one real (synthetic) example from each of the three sample lender profiles.

How a lender engages with us

Steps 1–5 are free, full stop — not a discounted trial. You don't need to be doing a deal to get value from this: a clean, WCDS-mapped loan tape improves your own CRMS submissions, credit bureau reporting, and IFRS 9 inputs, whether or not you ever raise funding through Waterline. Step 6 is the only point where a commercial conversation starts, and only if you want it to.

01

Discovery & NDA

Share a sample extract from your loan management system — a few hundred rows is enough. NDA first, no commitment, no cost.

02

Data readiness assessment

We profile your sample against WCDS: column coverage, null rates, type mismatches, duplicate keys. You get a gap report and a data-quality score before anything is mapped.

03

Mapping & standardisation

Our tooling proposes a field-by-field mapping from your source system to WCDS. Every transformation rule is named, versioned, and shown to you — nothing is silently reinterpreted.

04

Validation & reconciliation

The full rulebook runs against your whole book. Exceptions come back to you with the exact record, field, and rule that failed — not just a rejected file.

05

Clean WCDS dataset delivered

A versioned, audit-ready canonical loan tape — yours to keep and reuse for CRMS, bureau, or IFRS 9 reporting, independent of anything else you do with us.

06

Ready for structuring

When you are ready to raise funding, the same prepared dataset supports pool construction and investor diligence without starting again.

Loan book clean-up, as a service

Technology-assisted, not a manual spreadsheet exercise. This is what the mapping/validation/reconciliation pipeline above actually does for you.

Assisted field mapping

Deterministic rules for clean exports; assisted column-matching for messy, inconsistently-named source files. You approve every mapping before it's applied — nothing is guessed silently.

Deterministic validation

The WCDS rulebook (30 rules and counting) runs against your full book: required fields, domain ranges, date ordering, DPD/bucket consistency, default and write-off integrity, duplicate-key detection.

Reconciliation to your own totals

Facility counts and monetary control totals are checked against the source system's own declared totals — so you know nothing was dropped or double-counted in transit.

PII stays yours

BVN, NIN, TIN, phone, email, address and DOB are classified and masked in every non-operational export. Raw identifiers never appear in an analytics, investor, or funding dataset.

Full lineage, always

Every canonical or derived value traces back to its source record and the exact transformation rule/version that produced it. Corrections create new versions — nothing is silently overwritten.

Multi-destination export

One clean WCDS dataset, multiple outputs: CRMS-shaped, credit-bureau-shaped, IFRS 9-shaped, or an investor tape — each hashed and versioned at export.
