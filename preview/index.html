Credit infrastructure from raw loan data to investable structure.

Waterline helps Nigerian lenders prepare their portfolios for institutional funding and gives capital providers a clearer, more disciplined way to assess credit.

Platform

One controlled workflow.

Each part of Waterline reduces a specific source of delay or uncertainty in private-credit execution.

01

Market context

Comparable public information supports lender screening, sector analysis and transaction origination.

02

Data readiness

Loan tapes are mapped into one consistent model, checked against clear rules and reconciled to source totals.

03

Deal structuring

Eligible pools, advance rates, tranches and payment priorities are evaluated from the prepared dataset.

Process

From first review to ongoing reporting.

01Assess

Review the lender, book and funding objective.

02Standardise

Map fields and normalise values.

03Validate

Identify exceptions and reconcile totals.

04Structure

Build the eligible pool and funding stack.

05Operate

Support closing, monitoring and reporting.
