How the deal works

Built so everyone stays motivated to get paid back.

Here's a real example: a ₦1 billion loan pool, split into two simple pieces.

Senior notes ₦750M
75% of the pool16% returnPaid first
Lender's stake ₦250M
25% of the poolKept by the lenderAbsorbs losses first

Investors get paid first. The lender's own money is on the line before anyone else's — so they stay locked in on collecting every repayment.

The lender keeps upside, not just risk. They stay on as the loan servicer, keep the relationship with borrowers, and earn the biggest return in the structure — if the pool performs.

Why now

Nigeria just opened this door. We're walking through it early.

New SEC rules from 2025 made it clearer than ever for private companies to raise debt this way. The mechanics work — what's been missing is someone who structures the deal well and sticks around to run it.
