Terms of Service

Waterline Collections · last updated 10 October 2026

1. The service

Waterline Collections sends polite reminders about balances you upload, offers your customers instalment plans you set, and helps them pay directly into your bank account. We don't hold or move your money.

Waterline Collections is a service of 3D7 Technologies Limited (RC 6979200), a company registered in Nigeria with its office in Lagos. Call or WhatsApp +234 809 410 4100, or email collections@waterline.ng.

2. Verification

We work with organisations in the sectors we've opened (currently schools and estates; lenders and others to follow). Your account starts as Pending. We verify each organisation manually — for lenders, including their licence or registration — before any message is sent and may reject or suspend an account at our discretion, including for complaints, unusually high dispute or opt-out rates, or unpaid invoices.

3. Your promises

  • Every balance you upload is accurate, genuinely owed to you, and you are entitled to contact the person about it.
  • You upload only the person who owes — never guarantors, relatives, employers or children.
  • You check your bank and confirm or reject proofs of payment promptly (within 5 working days), and resolve queries fairly.
  • You tell us if a balance is paid, reduced or withdrawn outside the platform.

4. How we contact people

Message wording is fixed by Waterline. We never threaten, shame, mention police, courts, arrest, blacklisting or a child's schooling, and never contact third parties. Messages go out at most once a day and twice in any 7 days, and stop when someone opts out or raises a query.

5. Fees

There is no setup or monthly fee. You pay a success fee (shown in your account; 20% by default for schools and estates, or a rate you propose and we approve in writing, which may vary with how long a debt has been overdue) on payments you confirm, where we had contacted the person before they paid and the payment came within the attribution window (60 days by default) of our most recent message to them, however they paid — including cash or transfers made directly to you, such as at your office or bursary. Payments recorded on withdrawn accounts don't attract a fee. We invoice monthly; invoices are due within 14 days. We may pause outreach on accounts with invoices more than 30 days overdue.

6. Ending the service

You can stop at any time. Fees for payments already confirmed remain due. You can export your data from Settings, and ask us to delete it.

7. Liability

We don't guarantee that any balance will be recovered. To the extent the law allows, our total liability is limited to the fees you paid us in the previous 3 months.

Data Processing Agreement

For customer data you upload, you are the data controller and Waterline is your data processor under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. Waterline will:

  • process customer data only to provide the service and on your documented instructions;
  • keep it confidential, limit staff access to those who need it, and encrypt contact and bank details at rest;
  • never contact anyone other than the person on the record, and never use the data for any other purpose;
  • help you respond to people exercising their data rights;
  • tell you without undue delay, and within 72 hours, of any personal data breach affecting your data;
  • delete or anonymise customer data 12 months after a balance closes, or sooner on your request, and return it to you (via export) on request;
  • keep an audit log of approvals, uploads, messages, confirmations, disputes and invoices, available to you on request.

You confirm you have a lawful basis to share the customer data with us for this purpose and that you have told your customers you may use a collections partner.
