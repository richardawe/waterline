How we behave

  • We never threaten, shame or contact anyone other than the person who owes — no guarantors, relatives, employers or references, ever.
  • We never message children.
  • No more than one message a day, and no more than two in any week. Anyone can reply STOP to end messages for good.
  • Only you, your teammates and the Waterline team handling your account can see your list.

Questions

Who sends the messages?

Waterline's team sends each message on your behalf, in your name, from a Waterline WhatsApp number (and by email where you've given us an address). The wording is fixed by us and always polite. Every message says it's from you via Waterline and how to stop messages.

How do parents, residents and customers pay?

Each message has a private link. It shows what they owe, lets them pay in full or pick an instalment plan, and shows your bank details so they pay straight into your account. They then upload their receipt.

What if someone says they've already paid?

They can tell us on the same link, and messages to them pause straight away. You check your records and either confirm the balance, correct it, or close it.

How is the fee charged?

On payments you confirm arrived within 60 days of our last message to that person, however they paid — bank transfer, or cash at your office or bursary. Payments that come in before we've contacted someone, or more than 60 days after our last message, are free. At the start of each month we send one invoice for the previous month's confirmed recoveries: 20% of what came in, or the rate we've agreed with you.

Can we start with old arrears?

Yes, and it's the best place to start. Balances you've already written off cost you nothing to try: if nobody pays, you pay nothing.

Can we negotiate the fee?

Yes. Propose a rate when you sign up, or any time from Settings. Our team reviews it and lets you know; an approved rate applies to payments you confirm from then on.

Who can see our list?

Only you, your teammates, and the Waterline team handling your account. We never share it with other clients or anyone else.

How is our data protected?

We only collect what's needed to recover a balance. Phone numbers, emails and bank details are encrypted, only you and the Waterline team handling your account can see your list, and we delete or anonymise people's details 12 months after their balance is closed. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. See our privacy policy.
