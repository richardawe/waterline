Questions

Who sends the messages?

Waterline's team sends each message on your behalf, in your name, from a Waterline WhatsApp number (and by email where you've given us an address). The wording is fixed by us and always polite — every message says it's from you via Waterline and how to stop messages.

How do parents, residents and customers pay?

Each message has a private link. It shows what they owe, lets them pay in full or pick an instalment plan, and shows your bank details so they pay straight into your account. They then upload their receipt.

What if someone says they've already paid?

They can tell us on the same link, and messages to them pause straight away. You check your records and either confirm the balance, correct it, or close it.

How is the fee charged?

Only on payments you confirm arrived, after we've contacted that person. At the start of each month we send one invoice for the previous month's confirmed recoveries — 20% of what came in, or the rate we've agreed with you.

Can we negotiate the fee?

Yes. Propose a rate when you sign up, or any time from Settings. Our team reviews it and lets you know; an approved rate applies to payments you confirm from then on.

Do you work with lenders?

Lenders — microfinance banks, digital lenders, finance companies and cooperatives — are coming soon. Join the waitlist below and we'll get in touch as soon as we open.

How is our data protected?

We only collect what's needed to recover a balance. Phone numbers, emails and bank details are encrypted, only you can see your list, and we delete or anonymise people's details 12 months after their balance is closed. We follow the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023. See our privacy policy.
