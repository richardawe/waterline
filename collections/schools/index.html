How it works

  1. Upload your list. A spreadsheet of who owes what: name, phone number, amount and what it's for. Start with the old balances you've given up on.
  2. We send polite reminders. On WhatsApp, in your name, at most one a day and never more than two a week. Every parent gets a private link to see the balance and pay.
  3. They pay you directly. Straight into your bank account, in full or by instalments you set. They upload the receipt; you confirm it arrived.
  4. You pay only on results. 20% of what you confirm you recovered within 60 days of our last message, however they paid. Nothing upfront.

Questions

Will parents know the school is using a collection service?

Yes. Every message says it's from your school via Waterline. The wording is warm and polite, and never mentions a child's schooling, results or being sent home.

Can we include fees from previous sessions?

Yes. Old arrears are the best place to start: if nobody pays, you pay nothing.

How much does it cost?

Nothing upfront and no monthly fee. You pay 20% (or a rate we agree with you) of payments you confirm arrived within 60 days of our last message to that person, however they paid. If nobody pays, you pay nothing.

What about parents who pay cash at the bursary?

Record it in your dashboard and messages to them stop. Cash paid within 60 days of our last message still counts as recovered, because the reminder did its job.

Who sees the parents' details?

Only you, your teammates and the Waterline team handling your account. Details are encrypted and handled under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023.
