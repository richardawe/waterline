Privacy policy

Waterline Collections · last updated 10 October 2026

Who we are

Waterline Collections ("we") helps businesses such as schools, estates, gyms and service providers follow up politely on unpaid balances. This policy explains what personal data we handle and why, under the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 (NDPA).

Two kinds of people

Our clients are the businesses who sign up. For them we act as a data controller for their account details (business name, contact person's name, email, phone, role, location and the bank account their customers pay into).

The people who owe our clients ("customers"). Our client is the controller of their data; we process it on the client's instructions under a Data Processing Agreement.

What we collect about customers

Only what's needed to recover a balance: name, phone number, optional email, what the balance is for, the amount, an optional due date and, for schools, an optional first name of the child the fees are for (never a surname, date of birth or photo). If you use your payment page, we also keep what you send us: the amount and date you paid, the name on the transfer, your receipt, and any query you raise.

We do not accept details of anyone else — no guarantors, relatives, employers or references — and we never contact anyone other than the person who owes.

How we use it

  • To send polite reminders on the client's behalf, by WhatsApp, SMS or email, between 8am and 7pm on Monday to Saturday (never on public holidays), no more than once a day and twice a week.
  • To show you your balance and payment options on your private link.
  • To pass your receipts and queries to the business so they can confirm or correct your balance.
  • To calculate our fee to the client and keep an audit trail.

We never share your details with other businesses, publish them, report them to credit bureaus, or use them for marketing.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message, or press "Stop messages" on your payment page, and we stop straight away. Your page stays available so you can still pay.

Security and retention

Phone numbers, emails, names and bank details are encrypted at rest. Receipts are stored privately and only shown through links that expire after 15 minutes. Each business sees only its own customers. We delete or anonymise customer data 12 months after a balance is closed, and payment links stop working 30 days after it closes.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, or object to how it's used, by emailing collections@waterline.ng. If you're a customer, we'll involve the business that holds your balance. You may also complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.
